# Contoso Internal Policies (Sample)

## 1. Leave Policy
- Employees accrue **1.5 days of paid leave per month**.
- Unused leave can be carried forward up to **18 days** at year end.
- Sick leave: up to **10 days per year**, requires medical certificate if more than 2 consecutive days.

## 2. Remote Work Policy
- Remote work is permitted up to **3 days per week** with manager approval.
- Employees must be available online between **10:00–17:00 local time** on remote days.
- Work from outside the country requires HR approval.

## 3. Expense Reimbursement
- Meals: reimbursed up to **$25/day** with receipt.
- Travel: economy class for flights under 6 hours; business class requires VP approval.
- Submit expenses within **30 days** of purchase.

## 4. Security & Access
- Do not share credentials. MFA is mandatory.
- Report suspected phishing immediately to Security Operations.
